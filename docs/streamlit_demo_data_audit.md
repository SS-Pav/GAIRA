## Per-tab mapping

### Tab 1 — Methods / Pipeline
- **Hero** — `figures/gaira_three_phase_master_figure.png`
- **Corpus counters** — `grounding_corpus_summary.csv`, `atlas_explorer.csv`
- **Family bar** — `grounding_family_counts.csv`
- **Axis-coverage stacked bar** — `atlas_axis_coverage.csv`
- **Band ruler** — `atlas_explorer.csv` (hover: range, class, candidates, ambiguity, locality, source count)

### Tab 2 — Grounding Results
- **Spectra overlay** — `grounding_molecule_spectra.parquet` (min-max normalized display)
- **BSV bar + radar** — `grounding_molecule_bsv.csv` (BSV, not ΔBSV — pure molecules have no baseline)
- **Context table** — merge of `grounding_molecule_bsv.csv` + `grounding_molecule_index.csv`

### Tab 3 — Calibration Results
- **Bar per contrast** — `calibration_delta_bsv.csv`, axes grey-shaded where `testable == False`
- **Heatmap** — same table, reshaped (contrasts × canonical axes)
- **Radar overlay** — same table, |ΔBSV| per axis
- **Summary pills** — `calibration_conditions.csv` (`overall_label`, `confidence_weighted_score`)
- **No spectra in this tab** — by design.

### Tab 4 — Regression / Dose-response Explorer
- **Radar (baseline vs current)** — `ergothioneine_bsv_per_concentration.csv`
- **ΔBSV bars** — same table, `delta_bsv_*` columns
- **Dose curve** — `ergothioneine_dose_response.csv` (primary axis) + commit fraction from `ergothioneine_bsv_per_concentration.csv`
- **Slider marker** — vline on dose curve

## Honesty notes

- BSV vs ΔBSV distinction is respected: Tab 2 shows **BSV contribution only**, Tab 3 and Tab 4 show **ΔBSV**.
- In Tab 3, axes where SAEL direction is `unknown` are visually shaded and excluded from scoring pills.
- In Tab 4, "commit fraction" uses a simple |Δ|>0.005 threshold; below that, axes are called flat — consistent with v3's `below noise` verdict.
- No exact-molecule claims are made; dominant-axis summaries describe spectral projection only.
